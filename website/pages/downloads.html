How to start playing

  1. Create an account on this website if you have not yet, and make your first character.
  2. Download our client with the button above and extract the zip anywhere you like, for example your Desktop.
  3. Start the client from the extracted folder, choose Valdoria, and log in with your account name and password.

Trouble starting the client? Make sure you extracted the whole zip first, then ask the team for help.
