1. Names
a) No names that are insulting, racist, sexist, extremist or otherwise offensive.
b) No names made of sentences, random letters or odd formatting.
c) No names of real celebrities or countries, and no names that pretend to be another player or a member of the team.

2. Cheating
a) Do not exploit bugs, for example to duplicate items. If you find one, report it to the team right away.
b) Do not abuse weaknesses in the gameplay, such as blocking others with items or players so they cannot move.
c) Do not use bots, macros or any tool that plays for you.
d) Do not modify the client or use extra software to gain an advantage.
e) Do not try to steal other players' accounts.
f) Play on one character at a time; no multi-clienting.
g) Do not share, sell or trade accounts.

3. The team
a) Do not threaten or harass members of the team.
b) Do not pretend to be a member of the team.
c) Do not give the team false information or make false reports.

4. Player killing
a) Do not kill players excessively who carry no skull.

Breaking these rules can lead to a temporary or permanent ban of your characters and account, decided by the team based on how serious it was and on your history.

The rules may change at any time; changes are announced on this website.
