Type these in the default chat channel while you are in game.

CommandWhat it does
!onlineLists the players who are online right now.
!serverinfoShows the experience, skill, magic and loot rates.
!uptimeShows how long the server has been running.
!buyhouseBuys the house whose door you are facing.
!sellhouse NameOffers your house to another player.
!leavehouseGives up the house you are standing in.
!changesexChanges your character between male and female.
!deathlist NameShows how a character died recently.
!killsShows your unjustified kills and when your skull runs out.
